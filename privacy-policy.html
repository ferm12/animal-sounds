Privacy Policy

Animal Sound App · Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Animal Sound App ("the App," "we," "us") handles information. We built this App to be simple and kid-friendly, and that extends to how we handle privacy: we do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information We Collect

The App does not require an account, and does not collect any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, or location.

The App stores one small piece of information locally on your own device: whether you have unlocked the Safari Animals section. This value never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock the Safari Animals section. This purchase is processed entirely by Apple's App Store (or Google Play, on Android) using their own in-app purchase systems. We do not receive, see, or store your payment information, such as credit card numbers — that is handled directly by Apple/Google under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple's Privacy Policy
  • Google's Privacy Policy

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any in the first place.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

Data Security

Since the App does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal data at risk of being exposed through our systems. The one locally-stored preference described above stays on your device and is governed by your device's own security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
